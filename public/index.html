Cartoon Snap!

The fastest multiplayer card game.

How to Play 🎮

  • 🃏 Matching: Take turns playing cards to the center pile.
  • ⚡ Snapping: When two matching characters appear, hit SNAP!
  • ❌ False Snap Penalties: Hitting SNAP when there's no match skips your next turn!
